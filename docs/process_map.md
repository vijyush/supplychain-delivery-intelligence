# Process map — order fulfilment (AS-IS -> TO-BE)

## AS-IS
1. Lateness only known after the fact (reactive).
2. Shipping mode chosen without risk insight.
3. Loss-making orders spotted late in finance review.

## TO-BE
1. Every order scored for late-delivery risk AT ORDER TIME.
2. High-risk orders re-routed / expedited before dispatch.
3. Margin and loss-order dashboards flag unprofitable lines early.
4. Demand forecast feeds inventory/capacity planning.

**Gap analysis:** shift lateness from reactive to predicted; surface margin leaks
proactively; add demand forecasting for planning.
